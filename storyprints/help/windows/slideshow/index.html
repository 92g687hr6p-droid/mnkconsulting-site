Creating a slideshow

Chapter 8 of 10 · 5 scenes · about 65 seconds

0:00

    The steps in this chapter

    1. A folder for the showA slideshow plays one folder of photos. In File Explorer, make a new folder for the show, and copy in the photos you want. They can come from StoryPrints or anywhere else. StoryPrints never changes them: it only adds one small file with the show’s order and captions.
    2. Open the folderShows start from the home screen: Slideshow, under Show your photos. The Slideshow screen explains: the photos for a show go in a folder of their own. Choose that folder, and click New show.
    3. The show’s settingsThe bar along the top sets the whole show: how long each photo stays, fade or cut between photos, whether to start again at the end, what the caption says, how the date is shown, and the order. Sort by date or by name, or drag a photo to a new place. Choose how dates show (here, just the year), tick Start again to keep it repeating, name the show, and click Save.
    4. One photo’s own settingsClick a photo to set its own time, caption or date style. Change its words here for this show only: the photo itself isn’t changed. Or hide it: it stays in the folder but is left out of the show.
    5. A title page, then playTitle & PDF sets a title page with your own words and a cover photo. Tick Show it first in the slideshow to start the show with it. Click Play. The show fills the screen, with each photo’s caption underneath. Space pauses, the arrow keys go back and forward, and Esc stops.

    Order, timing and captions are kept in a small show file in the show’s folder; the photos are never changed. New photos copied into the folder later are added at the end, marked New.
