Album pages, and the details

Chapter 6 of 10 · 2 scenes · about 30 seconds

0:00

    The steps in this chapter

    1. Take photos from a pageOn an album page, the line at the top says what to do: click a photo on the page. StoryPrints finds the photo and opens it on the crop screen. Adjust it if needed, then click Apply Crop. Back on the page it’s numbered and shown under the page. Take the next photo the same way. Click a photo under the page to open it: turn it, change its crop or add its details.
    2. Description, date and file nameType what you know: who, where, and the story behind it. For the date, start with the year, then the month and day, only if you know them. The month is chosen from a list. Give the photo your own file name, or leave it and StoryPrints names it when you save. Save & next goes on to the next photo.

    The description and date are saved inside the photo, so other apps can show them: in Windows they appear as the photo’s Title and Date taken. You can come back to an album page later and take more photos from it.
