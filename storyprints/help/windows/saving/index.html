Saving

Chapter 7 of 10 · 3 scenes · about 35 seconds

0:00

    The steps in this chapter

    1. Finish, and choose where to saveWhen you’re ready, click Finish & save. Save to shows where the photos will go. Choose folder picks a different one, then click Save. Each finished photo is saved there, with its description and date.
    2. Copies, never your originalsStoryPrints always saves new copies. The photos you added are never changed. Save the same photo again and you get another file, such as Brighton pier 1962 (1). When you’re happy with the results, you can delete the originals yourself, or keep them, for example to take more photos from an album page later.
    3. Save progress, carry on laterA big project? Save progress keeps it, so you can stop and carry on later. Give the work a name. Photos ready so far are saved, and everything else is kept in the same folder. Next time, it’s on the home screen under Continue saved work, click Continue, and everything is just as you left it.

    Saved work keeps its own folder: a photo saved before and changed since is saved over its earlier copy, and your original photos are never touched.
