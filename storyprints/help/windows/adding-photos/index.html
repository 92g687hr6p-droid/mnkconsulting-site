Adding your photos

Chapter 4 of 10 · 4 scenes · about 45 seconds

0:00

    The steps in this chapter

    1. Add photosThe Add photos tile tells you how: Ctrl-click to pick several photos at once. Click Add photos, then say whether they’re single photos or album pages. Pick the photos, holding Ctrl to choose more than one, and click Open. They’re added, ready to crop. Add more from other folders the same way.
    2. Photo or album page? Change itAdded something as the wrong type? Open it and click the ⋮ menu at the top right. It's an album page turns it into a page you can take photos from. On an album page, It's a photo changes it back.
    3. Digital photos: no crop, just the storyPhotos that are already digital don’t need cropping. Click No crop. A photo is only saved if you change something. Click Details to add its description and as much of the date as you know, and it’s ready to save.
    4. The review gridIn the grid, an amber edge and a Crop label mean a photo still needs cropping. A green tick means it’s ready to save. Click Needs a crop to see just those still to do. All shows everything again.

    iPhone photos (HEIC) open once Windows’ HEIF Image Extensions and HEVC Video Extensions are installed from the Microsoft Store. Photos that can’t be opened are named, with the reason.
