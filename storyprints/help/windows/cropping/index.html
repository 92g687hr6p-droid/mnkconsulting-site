Cropping single prints

Chapter 5 of 10 · 3 scenes · about 45 seconds

0:00 / 0:15

    The steps in this chapter

    1. A good crop: accept itStoryPrints finds the edges for you. If the lime outline is right, click Accept crop. To accept lots at once, choose Select photos in the grid, click the good ones, then click Accept crops.
    2. Missed an edge? Click near itIf an edge is out, click near where the edge really is. StoryPrints looks there and snaps the line to it. Try this first: it’s often all you need.
    3. Fine adjustmentDrag a corner or side to move it. For fine control, click a corner, or press 1 to 4, then nudge it with the arrow keys while the magnifier shows it close up. The mouse wheel zooms, and Ctrl+Z undoes. When it’s right, click Apply & next.

      Full photo (or the F key) uses the whole picture, for a photo that was cropped already. Undo crop, on the photo’s own screen, goes back to the original. The Shortcuts panel on the crop screen lists every key.
