Cropping single prints

Chapter 5 of 10 · 4 scenes · about 55 seconds

0:00

    The steps in this chapter

    1. A good crop: accept itStoryPrints finds the edges for you and outlines the photo in lime. If the outline is right, click Accept crop. The photo is cropped and ready to save.
    2. Accept several at onceTo accept several crops at once, press and hold one of them in the grid. Click the others whose outlines look right, then click Accept crops. They’re all cropped and ready to save.
    3. Missed an edge? Click near itShortcuts, at the top right, lists what the mouse and keys can do. If a side of the box is in the wrong place, click near the edge. Close the list, then click just inside the photo, near its real edge. StoryPrints finds the edge there and moves that side to it. Try this first: it’s often all you need. Then click Apply Crop.
    4. Fine adjustmentOpen Shortcuts to see the mouse and keys for fine adjustment: drag a corner, choose a corner with 1 to 4, move it with the arrow keys, and zoom with the mouse wheel. Close the list, then drag a corner. The magnifier shows it close up. Press 1 to 4 to choose a corner, then nudge it with the arrow keys. The mouse wheel zooms in where the pointer is, and 0 zooms out again. When it’s right, click Apply Crop.

    Ctrl+Z (or Undo) undoes the last change. Full photo (or the F key) uses the whole picture, for a photo that was cropped before. Undo crop, on the photo’s own screen, goes back to the original. The keys shown on screen in this guide are only there to show what was pressed.
