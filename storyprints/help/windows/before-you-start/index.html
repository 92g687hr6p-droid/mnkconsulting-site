    The steps in this chapter

    1. StoryPrints for WindowsOld prints, album pages and digital photos, brought together as one collection, each with its story.
    2. What you start withYou might have family albums, a box of loose prints, and photos that friends and relatives have emailed or sent. StoryPrints works with all three.
    3. What you end up withAt the end you have one folder of digital photos, each with its description and date saved inside it. From that folder you can make a slideshow, and a PDF to share or print. The photos stand on their own: any photo app can open them, with or without StoryPrints.

    StoryPrints for Windows works on your own PC, with photos you copy from your phone or receive from family. It never changes your original photos.
