Creating a PDF

Chapter 9 of 10 · 3 scenes · about 40 seconds

0:00

    The steps in this chapter

    1. A4 pagesMake a PDF from the same show: open Title & PDF. Choose A4 pages, and 1, 2 or 4 photos per page. Click Make PDF. It’s saved beside the photos, with the title page first and each photo’s caption under it.
    2. An A5 booklet to printOr choose A5 booklet, with 1 or 2 photos on each page. The note explains the two PDFs: the A5 pages, and a booklet to print on both sides of A4. Print the test sheet first to check your printer. Then Make PDF, print the booklet on both sides, flipping on the short edge, fold the sheets in half and staple on the fold.
    3. Long captionsA printed page has less room than the screen. StoryPrints tells you which captions are too long. Review shows each one, with the part that won’t print crossed out. Shorten it automatically, or write shorter words just for the PDF. The photo’s own description isn’t changed.

    Double-sided matte inkjet paper of about 170–230 gsm feels like a book, with card for the cover; plain paper is fine for a draft. PDFs are always new files, saved beside the photos.
