Copying your photos to the PC

Chapter 3 of 10 · 1 scene · about 15 seconds

0:00

    The steps in this chapter

    1. Copy the photos to your PCPhotos of prints and album pages go to the PC the same way. With the phone’s USB cable, on Android choose File transfer, on an iPhone tap Trust, or use the Windows Photos app’s Import. Your own OneDrive camera backup works too. iPhone photos need Windows’ HEIF and HEVC extensions. Avoid Google Photos Storage saver, which lowers the quality. On an iPhone, if the photos were taken as Live Photos, turn Live off for them before you copy them.

    Photos copied by cable keep their dates and details. StoryPrints itself never uses the internet to move them.
