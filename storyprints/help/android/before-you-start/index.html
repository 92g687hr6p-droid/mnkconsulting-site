    The steps in this chapter

    1. What you start with, and end withStoryPrints turns old prints, album pages and photos already on your phone into neatly cropped photos, each with its description and date. You photograph them, check the crops, add the details, and save them to your phone’s gallery.
    2. The home screenEverything starts from the home screen. It shows how many photos you have, how many are ready to save and how many still need a crop. Review & crop takes you through them; Finish & save saves them. Help, at the top, opens these guides.
    3. Stop now, carry on laterYou don’t have to finish in one go. Tap Save progress to keep everything, even photos not yet cropped. Next time, it’s waiting under Continue saved work. You can even continue on a PC: chapter 9 shows how.

    Slideshows and the printed booklet are made on a PC with StoryPrints for Windows. Chapter 9 shows how to carry your work across.
