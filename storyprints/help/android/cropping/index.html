Cropping

Chapter 5 of 9 · 3 scenes · about 40 seconds

0:00

    The steps in this chapter

    1. A good crop: accept itStoryPrints finds the edges for you and outlines the photo in lime. If the outline is right, tap Accept crop. The photo is cropped and ready to save. Use the arrows at the top to move to the next photo.
    2. Missed an edge? Tap near itIf an edge is out, tap Adjust. Then tap just inside the photo, close to where its edge really is. StoryPrints looks there and snaps the line to it. Try this first, as it’s often all you need. If it says No edge there, drag the line instead. Then tap Apply Crop.
    3. Drag, and fine-tuneYou can also drag a corner or a side. A magnifier shows it close up under your finger. For fine control, tap the sliders button at the top right. Its buttons move the whole outline, or one edge in or out, in coarse or fine steps. Undo steps back, and Zoom takes a closer look. When it’s right, tap Apply Crop.

    Full photo, in the precision tools, uses the whole picture, for photos already cropped. Undo crop, on the photo’s own screen, goes back to the original. When you crop with Crop next, in the grid, the button reads Apply & next and moves straight on.
