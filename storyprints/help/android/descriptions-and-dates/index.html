Descriptions, dates and file names

Chapter 7 of 9 · 3 scenes · about 35 seconds

0:00

    The steps in this chapter

    1. Add a descriptionTap Add a description and date, under any photo. Say who’s in it, where it was and what was happening. The description is saved inside the photo, so it goes wherever the photo goes.
    2. Dates: as much as you knowStart with the year, then add the month and day if you know them. Leave out anything you don’t know. A year on its own is saved as the first of July, and a month without a day as the fifteenth, so your photos still sort into roughly the right order in other apps.
    3. Your own file nameIf you’d like the saved photo to have a name of your own, type it under File name. Leave it empty and StoryPrints names it when you save. Tap Save & next to move straight on to the next photo’s details.

    Month opens once there’s a year, and Day once there’s a month. Save & next appears when there’s another photo after this one; on the last photo, tap Save.
