Album pages

Chapter 3 of 9 · 3 scenes · about 40 seconds

0:00

    The steps in this chapter

    1. Photographing the pageFor an album, switch the camera to Album pages. Leave the prints where they are: there’s no need to take them out. If the plastic film makes glare, peel it back. Get as much of the page in as you can, with a little space round it. A table round the edge is fine.
    2. Tap each photo you wantOpen the page and tap a photo on it. StoryPrints finds its edges and shows you the crop. Adjust it if you need to, then tap Apply Crop. Each photo you take gets a green box with a number. Tap inside a green box to crop that photo again.
    3. Wrong type? Change itTook an album page as a print? Open it and tap the ⋮ menu at the top right. Choose It’s an album page. On an album page, It’s a photo changes it back. If a photo is blurred, Retake replaces it with a new one.

    The page itself isn’t saved: only the photos you take from it. It’s a photo only works before you’ve taken any photos from the page. Taken a photo you didn’t mean to? Open it from the strip below the page and choose Undo crop.
