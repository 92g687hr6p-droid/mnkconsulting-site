Taking photos of prints

Chapter 2 of 9 · 3 scenes · about 40 seconds

0:00

    The steps in this chapter

    1. Light, glare and shadowsDaylight is best: near a window, out of direct sun. A lamp or spotlight overhead makes glare on glossy prints, so turn it off. Watch for the shadow of your phone and hands. Hold the phone flat above the print. StoryPrints straightens photos taken at an angle, but flat is best.
    2. A backing, and a borderA backing that contrasts with the photo helps StoryPrints find the edges more easily: dark for light photos, light for dark ones. It isn’t essential. Leave a border of backing showing all round the print, so none of its edges is cut off.
    3. Taking the photosTap Take photos. Check that Prints is selected at the top, then tap the round button for each print. The last photo and the count show at the bottom, so you can keep going without stopping. When you’ve finished, tap Done.

    One print per photo. For several prints on a page, see Album pages, in chapter 3. Tap the last photo, at the bottom left, to check it.
