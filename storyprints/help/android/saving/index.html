Saving, and Save progress

Chapter 8 of 9 · 4 scenes · about 55 seconds

0:00

    The steps in this chapter

    1. Finish & saveWhen you’re ready, tap Finish & save. StoryPrints tells you how many photos it will save and where. Photos still to crop aren’t saved, so go back with Continue the session if you missed any. Tap Save. Your photos go to the gallery with their descriptions and dates, and you’re ready to start again.
    2. New copies, never overwritesStoryPrints always saves new copies. The photos you added are never changed. When you’re happy, you can delete the originals yourself, or keep them, for example to take more photos from an album page later. The description and date are saved inside each new photo.
    3. Save to a folder insteadTo keep them in a folder of their own, tap Choose folder and pick or make one inside Pictures or DCIM. StoryPrints remembers it for next time. Save to the photo gallery instead switches back.
    4. Save progressTo stop part way, tap Save progress. Give the work a name and choose a folder for it, such as a StoryPrints folder in Pictures. The photos that are ready are saved there, and everything else is kept beside them. Next time, tap Continue under Continue saved work.

    After the first time, Save progress just saves, to the same name and folder. With no file name of your own, a saved photo is named StoryPrints with the date and time; if a name is already used in a folder, Android adds a number, such as Brighton pier 1962 (1).
