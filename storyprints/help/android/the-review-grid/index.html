    The steps in this chapter

    1. What still needs a cropIn the grid, an amber edge and a Crop label mean a photo still needs cropping. A green tick means it’s ready to save. Tap Needs a crop to see just those still to do; All shows everything again. Tap a photo to open it, or tap Crop next to work through them one after another.
    2. Accept several at onceTo accept several crops at once, press and hold one of them in the grid. Tap the others whose outlines look right, then tap Accept crops. They’re all cropped and ready to save. The same way, No crop needed marks photos that need no cropping, and Remove takes them out of the session.

    Removed one by mistake? Undo, in the bar that appears, puts it back. The cross at the top left stops selecting. Album pages have their own section, with the photos taken from each.
