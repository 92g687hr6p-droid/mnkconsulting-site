StoryPrints for Android: how to

Short animated chapters, each about a minute. Watch them in order the first time, or jump to the one you need. Each one has the steps written out underneath too, and a Read aloud option under the player if you’d like the captions spoken, in a voice you choose.

  1. 1

    Before you start 3 scenes · about 35 seconds

    What StoryPrints on your phone is for, the home screen, and carrying on later.

  2. 2

    Taking photos of prints 3 scenes · about 40 seconds

    Daylight without glare or shadows, a contrasting backing with a border, and the camera.

  3. 3

    Album pages 3 scenes · about 40 seconds

    Photograph the whole page, tap each photo you want, and change the type.

  4. 4

    Photos you already have 3 scenes · about 40 seconds

    Photos from your library, the phone’s document scanner, and photos that need no crop.

  5. 5

    Cropping 3 scenes · about 40 seconds

    Accept a good crop, tap near a missed edge, drag a corner, and the precision tools.

  6. 6

    The review grid 2 scenes · about 30 seconds

    See what still needs a crop, and accept several crops at once.

  7. 7

    Descriptions, dates and file names 3 scenes · about 35 seconds

    The story behind each photo, saved inside it.

  8. 8

    Saving, and Save progress 4 scenes · about 55 seconds

    The gallery or a folder, new copies only, and carrying on later.

  9. 9

    Carry on on a PC 2 scenes · about 30 seconds

    Copy your saved work to a PC, and finish it in StoryPrints for Windows.

Making a slideshow or a printed booklet? That’s in StoryPrints for Windows.
