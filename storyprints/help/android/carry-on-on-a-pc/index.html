Carry on on a PC

Chapter 9 of 9 · 2 scenes · about 30 seconds

0:00

    The steps in this chapter

    1. Copy the folder to the PCTo carry on on a PC, first Save progress on the phone. Then plug in the phone’s USB cable and choose File transfer on the phone. In File Explorer, copy the whole folder your work is in to the PC. Keep the work’s file and its folder together.
    2. Carry on in StoryPrints for WindowsIn StoryPrints for Windows, click Open saved work from another folder and pick the work’s file. Everything comes back as you left it on the phone. Finish cropping with the mouse, then make your slideshow or booklet. The Windows help shows you how.

    Carry on in one place at a time; to take the work back to the phone, copy the folder back the same way. Only have finished photos, not saved work? Add them in StoryPrints for Windows as single photos, and mark them No crop needed.
