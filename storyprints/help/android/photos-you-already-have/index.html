Photos you already have

Chapter 4 of 9 · 3 scenes · about 40 seconds

0:00

    The steps in this chapter

    1. Add from libraryFor photos already on your phone, tap Add from library. Say whether they’re single photos or album pages. In the picker, press and hold the first photo to pick several, tap the others, then tap Add. Add more the same way.
    2. Scan a documentScan a document uses your phone’s own scanner, one print at a time. It finds the edges and straightens the print for you, so it comes back already cropped. To change the crop, open it in StoryPrints and tap Crop.
    3. Photos that need no cropDigital photos usually need no cropping. Select them in the grid and tap No crop needed. StoryPrints saves them only when you’ve changed something, so add a description and date, and the saved copy carries them.

    Pick single photos and album pages separately, as each batch is added as one type. Taking several prints is quicker with Take photos; the scanner is handy for one print that is hard to crop.
