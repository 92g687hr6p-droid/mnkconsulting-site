StoryPrints privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains how the StoryPrints app ("StoryPrints", "the app") handles your information. StoryPrints is published by MNK Consulting Limited ("we", "us"), a company registered in England and Wales.

In short: your photos and the stories you add stay on your device. StoryPrints has no account or sign-in, does not upload your photos, and we do not collect, receive or sell any of your personal information. The optional "Scan a document" feature uses a Google component that sends Google some technical information about the app and device, described below.

Your photos and descriptions

Everything you capture or import in StoryPrints is processed on your device. This includes finding the edges of photos, cropping and straightening them, and saving them. We have no servers that receive your photos, and StoryPrints does not send them over the internet.

Descriptions and dates you enter are written into each saved JPEG as standard photo metadata, so they travel with the image. If an imported photo already contains metadata, such as the date or the location where it was taken, StoryPrints keeps that metadata in the saved copy. If you share a saved photo, the people you share it with can read this metadata, as they can for any photo.

Finished photos are saved to your device's photo gallery. Working copies are kept in the app's temporary storage on your device while you work and are removed by the system or when you uninstall the app.

Permissions the app uses

  • Camera: to photograph prints and album pages. The camera is only used while you are capturing.
  • Saving to your gallery: to save finished photos where your other photo apps can see them.
  • Photo location metadata: to keep the location already stored in a photo you import. StoryPrints does not use your device's location.

When you add existing photos, you choose them with Android's own file picker. StoryPrints can only open the photos you pick and cannot browse the rest of your library.

The "Scan a document" feature (Google ML Kit)

"Scan a document" uses Google's ML Kit document scanner, provided through Google Play services on your device. The scanning itself happens on your device, and the images are not sent to Google. To run and improve the component, ML Kit sends Google limited technical information, such as:

  • device information (for example manufacturer, model and Android version);
  • the app's name and version;
  • a per-installation identifier that is not used to identify you or your device;
  • performance and error information, such as how long a scan took.

Google states that this information is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties. We do not receive it. You can avoid it by not using "Scan a document"; the rest of the app does not use ML Kit. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or profiles.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking in the app.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Google Play

If you download StoryPrints from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect information under its own privacy policy. Google may also share anonymous, aggregated statistics with us, such as numbers of installs and crash reports. These do not identify you.

Emailing us

If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We keep the correspondence only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Children

StoryPrints is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information from the app, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your photos are under your control on your device, and you can delete them at any time. If you have contacted us by email, you can ask for a copy of that correspondence or for it to be deleted. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and the date at the top of this page.
